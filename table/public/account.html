Account

Delete your account.

This is the LOOCHKA account used to open a table. Joining a friend’s table does not create one.

In the app

  1. Open LOOCHKA and sign in with the Google or Apple account that opens your tables.
  2. Open Settings, then Account.
  3. Choose Delete account and confirm.

Deleting it signs you out and closes every table you opened. You can sign in again later.

If you cannot open the app

Write to support@loochka.com from the email on the account and ask us to delete it. We close every table that account opened and sign it out.

Ask us to delete it

What is deleted

The sign-in for that account, and every table it still has open, including the names, chips, and moves on those tables. This happens when you confirm in the app, or when we handle the email request.

What is kept

If you signed in with Apple, the email and name Apple gave us stay, so a later sign-in can be recognized. A Google sign-in asks Google again; we do not keep a separate copy of that sign-in after deletion. A report stays, with the table, the seat, and the reason. A note sent to support stays so we can answer it. We may keep a record that a table was opened or a hand was played, tied to that table, so we can see when the game breaks. The ad network can keep that an ad was shown. We don’t sell this information. There is no extra retention period: what is deleted is removed at deletion, and what is kept stays for the reason above.
