Games

Two classics. One table.

♠ Up to 9 players

Texas Hold’em

A proper home game. Real seats, real blinds, and a dealer who never forgets whose turn it is.

  • Pick the small blind and the buy-in
  • Deal automatically, or when you’re ready
  • Buy back in when you run dry
  • See every stack in Statistics
One on one

Backgammon

Shesh-besh, tavla, tavli: whatever you call it, the board is set and the dice are warm.

  • Tap a checker to see where it can go
  • Doubling cube, gammons and backgammons
  • The rules are one tap away
  • A win log for the rematch
