Who runs this

LOOCHKA is run by its operator, reachable at support@loochka.com. The operator decides why and how the data described here is used, and is the controller under the EU GDPR and the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, as amended (Amendment 13).

Table rules

Chips are for the game. They are not money, and nothing here can be cashed out. A table is private: you need its ID and password. The name beside a seat is the one that player chose. You can report a name, a camera, or a microphone, and you can block a seat. We can close a table or remove a player who is making it unusable.

Your account

Opening a table needs a Google or Apple sign-in. Joining a friend’s table does not. You can delete the account from Settings. Deleting it signs you out and closes every table you opened. The steps, and what is removed or kept, are on the account page.

Privacy

If you sign in, we keep the email and name your Google or Apple account gives us, so we can recognize you the next time you open a table. At a table we keep the name you chose, the chips, and the moves, so the game can continue. Camera and microphone stay off until you turn them on, and they are only for the people at that table. A report includes the table, the seat, and the reason you picked. A note to support is used to answer you. A banner ad may appear at the table, and the ad network can tell that it was shown. We may record that a table was opened or a hand was played, tied to that table, so we can see when the game breaks. We don’t sell this information.

Why we use it

To run your account and the table (sign-in, seats, chips, moves, video) we rely on the contract with you: you asked for the service. To handle reports and blocks, prevent abuse, and see when the game breaks, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and working. Ads, and the identifiers the ad network uses, rely on your consent where the law requires it. You can change that choice at any time with “Ad privacy choices” at the bottom of the Legal screen in the app.

How long we keep it

  • A table, its chips and its moves: deleted 30 minutes after the last activity.
  • Your sign-in on a device: 7 days from when you last used it.
  • The email and name from your Google or Apple account: until you delete the account.
  • Reports and blocks: 90 days.
  • Usage events: tied to the table, not to your name or email, and kept by our analytics provider.
  • A note to support: as long as needed to answer you.

Who receives data

Service providers process data for us: Railway (hosting and database), Cloudflare (network), LiveKit (live video and audio between the people at a table), PostHog (usage analytics), and Google AdMob (ads). Some of them are outside the EU and Israel, mainly in the United States, and data may be transferred there under the providers’ standard contractual safeguards. We do not sell your data.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how it is used. You can delete your account in Settings, using the account page, or by writing to support@loochka.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority, such as the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

Children

The app is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has used it, write to support@loochka.com and we will delete the data.

Accessibility statement

If something in the app gets in the way, tell us what you were trying to do. We will answer, and when the screen itself cannot do it, we will arrange another way to get the same thing done. The standard we are working toward is Israeli Standard 5568, which follows WCAG 2.0 at level AA. A formal audit is not finished, so this is not a claim that every screen already meets that level. A few places we already know are harder: the poker and backgammon boards are drawn as a game, so a screen reader may not announce every chip or checker; camera and microphone have no text equivalent; banner ads come from another company.

To ask for an adjustment, or to tell us something isn’t usable, write to support@loochka.com.
