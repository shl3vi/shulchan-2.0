Questions

Quick answers.

Writing about a specific table? Include its ID so we can find it.

How do I open a table?

Sign in with Google or Apple, tap Create a new table, and pick Poker table or Backgammon table. Choose the name the table will see you by. For poker, also set the small blind and the buy-in. Then tap Deal me in.

Do my friends need an account?

No. Only the person who opens the table signs in. Friends open the link, or tap Join a table and type the ID and password, pick a name, and sit down.

Where are the table ID and password?

At the table, open the menu at the top right and choose Table info. Share table sends the ID and password together as one link.

The link didn’t open the app.

Make sure LOOCHKA is installed on that phone, then open the link again. Or open the app, tap Join a table, and type the ID and password from the message.

How many people can play?

A poker table has nine seats. Backgammon is one on one.

Is there real money?

No. Chips are for the game. They aren’t money, and nothing can be cashed out.

How do the camera and mic work?

Each seat has a camera button and a mic button. Both stay off until you turn them on, and only the people at that table can see and hear you.

Someone is ruining the table.

Tap their seat to report their name, camera or microphone, or to block the seat. The table admin can also remove a player from Admin options. We can close a table or remove a player who is making it unusable.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings, then Account, and choose Delete account. It signs you out and closes every table you opened. The full steps, and what is removed or kept, are on the account page.

Which languages does LOOCHKA speak?

English, עברית, Español, Русский and العربية.
