Using LOOCHKA

You must be at least 13 years old. A table is private: you need its ID and password, and you may only join tables you were invited to. The app is provided as it is, and we may change it, pause it or stop it. We do not promise it will always be available or error-free.

Your conduct

There is no tolerance for abusive content or behavior. Do not use a name, message, camera, microphone or any other part of the app to:

  • harass, bully, threaten or abuse anyone;
  • post hateful content or content that attacks people for who they are;
  • show or share sexual, violent or illegal content;
  • pretend to be another person, or use a name that impersonates someone;
  • cheat, or interfere with the game or with the service.

Video and voice

Camera and microphone are off until you turn them on, and they are only for the people at that table. Do not record, screenshot or share another player’s video or voice without their permission.

Reports, blocks and removal

You can report a name, a camera or a microphone, and you can block a seat; a block works both ways, so you stop seeing and hearing each other. We review reports and act on them within 24 hours. We may remove content, remove a player, or close a table that breaks these terms, without notice. Serious or repeated violations can mean losing access to the app.

Chips are not money

Chips are for the game. They have no value, nothing here can be bought with money or cashed out, and this is not gambling.

Your account

Opening a table needs a Google or Apple sign-in; joining a friend’s table does not. You are responsible for what happens at a table you open. You can delete your account at any time from Settings or on the account page. How we handle your data is described in the privacy policy.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms. When we make a material change, the app asks you to accept the new version before you continue. Questions, or something to report: support@loochka.com.
